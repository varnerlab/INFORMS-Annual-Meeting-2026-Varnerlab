## Caveats

- α is a name's average growth not explained by the market. Names differ in α by 0.089 per year on synthetic paths against 0.076 in real 2014–2024. A name's α carries over from 2014–2019 to 2020–2024 with correlation 0.13 on synthetic paths against 0.17 real, and from 2014–2024 into 2025–26 with 0.10 synthetic against −0.12 real.
- Each name keeps its 2014–2024 ranking by α. Its α is moved 40% of the way toward the average of all names, so it keeps 60% of its 2014–2024 distance from the average, and it does not drift over time. So this is a best case for picking by γ.
- Sector factors are drawn independently, so there are no rotations between sectors beyond what the market explains.
- Names move with the market on the same day only; no name leads or lags the market.
- The market model reproduces the regimes of 2014–2024. Worse regimes come only from rare draws, which the stress sets collect.
- The universe is fixed, as in the real run: no names enter or leave.
- Names whose β goes to 0 or below (the names the hedge can use): synthetic paths have a median of 7 in 2025–26 (range 1 to 19 across paths). Real periods of the same length within 2014–2024 had a median of 5 (range 0 to 53 across 112 overlapping periods, one ending every 21 trading days, about a month). The real period just before 2025 (2023-05-02 to 2024-12-31) had 53, and real 2025–26 had 120. So the synthetic paths look like a typical 2014–2024 period, while the real market already had more such names just before 2025 and many more in 2025–26.
- The results describe this generator plus these strategies, not a forecast.
